### California Housing Prices Analysis
**Research Question:** What factors most influence housing prices in California, and how does location affect property values?

**Visualization and Findings:**
![Income vs House Value](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RyanAnderson20048/data-science-project/main/plot2_income_value.png)
*Explanation:* The scatter plot clearly shows a strong positive correlation. As the median income of a block group increases, the median house value also increases significantly.

![Geographic Scatter](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RyanAnderson20048/data-science-project/main/plot1_geo_scatter.png)
*Explanation:* The geographic plot reveals that properties located near the coast (the left side of the plot) have notably higher house values (red dots) compared to inland areas.
